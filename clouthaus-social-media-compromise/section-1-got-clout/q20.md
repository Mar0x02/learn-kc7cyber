# Q20

**Pertanyaan:** What should you never reuse across different sites to protect your accounts?

**Konteks:**
Afomiya akhirnya sadar ada login attempt mencurigakan & Instagram-nya aneh. Dia langsung ganti password Instagram, update password di semua akun (gak reuse lagi), dan aktifin MFA di mana-mana. Cyber resilience = bangun habit (password kuat & unik, MFA di mana-mana, rutin cek login activity) sebelum hal buruk kejadian — attacker mengandalkan human behavior kayak reused password, ignoring alert, oversharing.
