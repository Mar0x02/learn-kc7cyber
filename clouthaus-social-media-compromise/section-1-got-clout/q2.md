# Q2

**Pertanyaan:** Which of the following signs should Afomiya look for to determine if an email offering a brand deal is a phishing attempt?

**Pilihan:**
- A. Urgent/threatening language ("Immediate action required", "Deadline in 24 hours")
- B. Minta info sensitif (password, credit card, SSN)
- C. Domain pengirim mencurigakan / gak match sama domain official brand-nya
- D. Generic greeting ("Dear Customer") bukan nyebut nama/titlenya langsung
- E. All of the above

**Konteks:**
Inbox Afomiya penuh brand deal (makeup, fitness gear, dll), tapi di antaranya nyelip phishing trap. Perlu tau red flag umum phishing email.

**Cara penyelesaian:** Semua poin A-D itu masing-masing emang red flag umum phishing email, jadi jawabannya gabungan semuanya.

**Jawaban:** E (All of the above)
