# Q5

**Pertanyaan:** What security measure saved Afomiya's email account from being hacked, despite the threat actor having access to her security question answers?

**Konteks:**
Attacker udah dapet semua jawaban security question yang bener ([Q4](./q4.md)), tapi kebentur satu hambatan: kode yang dikirim ke HP Afomiya. Gak ada kode, gak bisa akses — attacker gagal nembus lapisan keamanan terakhir ini.

**Cara penyelesaian:** Kode verifikasi tambahan ke HP (di luar password/security question) adalah ciri khas Multi-Factor Authentication.

**Jawaban:** MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication)
