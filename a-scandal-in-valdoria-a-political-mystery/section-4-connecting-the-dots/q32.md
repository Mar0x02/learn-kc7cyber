# Q32

**Pertanyaan:** Was data stolen from any other devices and uploaded to hirejob.com? (yes/no)

**Konteks:**
Query `ProcessEvents` buat semua device di The Valdorian Times.

**Cara penyelesaian:**
```kql
ProcessEvents
| where process_commandline contains "hirejob.com"
```
