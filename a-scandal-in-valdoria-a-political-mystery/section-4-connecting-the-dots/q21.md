# Q21

**Pertanyaan:** How many minutes elapsed between when the file was moved/renamed on Ronnie's machine and when the email was sent to Clark Kent?

**Cara penyelesaian:** Selisih antara [Q19](./q19.md) (`2024-01-31 10:26:20+00:00`, rename/move file) dan [Q20](./q20.md) (`2024-01-31 11:11:12+00:00`, email terkirim ke Clark Kent).

`11:11:12 - 10:26:20` = 2692 detik = 44 menit 52 detik.

**Jawaban:** 44
